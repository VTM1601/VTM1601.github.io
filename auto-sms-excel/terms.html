2. What the App does

Auto Message Sender helps you send a personal message to each person on a list. With the App you can:

  • import recipients and messages from an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file, pick recipients from your phone's contacts, or type their numbers yourself;
  • write one message for everyone with the recipient's name filled in, or a different message for each person;
  • send through SMS, WhatsApp or Telegram, optionally with photos or videos on WhatsApp;
  • schedule messages for a set time and get reminders;
  • see the history of each send and send missed or failed messages again.

The App does not send messages over its own network. It opens your messaging app, WhatsApp or Telegram with the recipient and message ready, and the message is sent by that app from your own phone number or account.

5. How messages are sent

5.1 SMS

The App never sends SMS by itself and does not request permission to do so. For each message it opens your messaging app with the number and text filled in, and you press Send.

5.2 WhatsApp and Telegram

For each send you choose how messages go out:

  • I press Send: the App opens the chat with the message ready and you press Send yourself.
  • Automatic: the App uses Android's accessibility service to press Send for you, waiting a random time between messages.

  • The accessibility service is optional and stays off until you turn it on, after the App explains what it does and you agree.
