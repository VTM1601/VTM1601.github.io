Privacy Policy

Auto SMS Excel · Effective 25 November 2026

Auto SMS Excel ("the app") is made by MANH VU TIEN ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone. We run no servers, we do not collect your data, and we do not show ads.

1. Information the app uses

The app only works with information you give it, and keeps it on your device:

  • Spreadsheet files (Excel or CSV) you choose: phone numbers or Telegram usernames, names, messages and send times.
  • Contacts, only if you tap "Choose from contacts" and allow access: the app reads contact names and phone numbers so you can pick recipients. It does not read anything else from your contacts.
  • Photos and videos you attach to WhatsApp messages: a copy is kept in the app's private storage until the send is deleted.
  • Send history: which messages were sent, skipped or failed, and when.
  • Settings: language, appearance, default country and your plan.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

2. How messages are sent

The app does not send SMS by itself and does not have permission to do so. For every message it opens your messaging app, WhatsApp or Telegram with the recipient and text filled in. The message is then sent by that app, under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • SMS: your mobile carrier and your messaging app.
  • WhatsApp: WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Telegram: Telegram Privacy Policy.

3. Accessibility service (Automatic sending)

If you choose Automatic sending and turn on the app's accessibility service, the app uses Android's Accessibility API to press Send in WhatsApp and Telegram for you. We show you a clear explanation and ask for your consent before you turn it on.

  • It works only inside WhatsApp and Telegram, and only while a send you started is running.
  • It reads the screen only to find the message box and the Send button, types the message you wrote (Telegram) and presses Send.
  • It does not record, store, collect or share anything it sees on the screen, and it does nothing in other apps.
  • You can turn it off at any time in your phone's Accessibility settings. Without it you can still send by pressing Send yourself.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you when scheduled messages are due.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) and run at startup: to remind you on time, also after the phone restarts.
  • Display over other apps: only to bring the app back after you press Send, so the next message can open.
  • Contacts: only to pick recipients, as described above.
  • Accessibility: only for Automatic sending, as described above.

5. Purchases

Premium is sold as a subscription through Google Play. Payments are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details; the app only learns whether your subscription is active.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. Because the app has no servers and no analytics or advertising tools, there is nothing for us to share.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete a send (with its messages and attachments) in History, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly process their information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published here with a new effective date, and important changes will be shown in the app.
