Privacy Policy

Auto Sending SMS · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Introduction

MANH VU TIEN ("we", "us" or "our") develops the Auto Sending SMS mobile application ("the App"). We respect your privacy and the privacy of the people you message. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

In short: your files, contacts, messages, attachments and send history stay on your phone. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We do not run servers for the App, and the App has no account sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking.

1. Information the App uses

The App works only with information you choose to give it. All of it is stored in the App's private storage on your phone.

1.1 Spreadsheet files

  • When you pick an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file, the App reads phone numbers or Telegram usernames, names, messages and send times from it.
  • The App reads only the file you pick, through Android's file picker. It cannot see your other files.
  • What it needs is saved in the App; the original file is not changed.

1.2 Phone contacts (optional)

  • Only if you tap "Choose from contacts" and allow access does the App read your contacts' names and phone numbers, to let you pick recipients.
  • It does not read emails, addresses, photos, notes or any other contact details, and it does not change your contacts.
  • Only the people you select are saved in the App, as recipients of that send.
  • If you do not allow access, everything else in the App still works.

1.3 Messages and send history

  • Message text, recipient names and numbers, scheduled times, and the status of each message (waiting, sent, skipped, failed) with dates.
  • When each message was sent (the recipient is stored only as a one-way code), used only to apply the daily sending limit.

1.4 Photos and videos (optional)

  • When you attach photos or videos to WhatsApp messages, they are picked with Android's photo picker, which shows the App only the items you select.
  • A copy of each item is kept in the App's private storage so it can be shared with WhatsApp when each message is sent. It is deleted when you delete the send.

1.5 Settings and purchase status

  • Language, appearance, default country, your choices for how to send, and whether the guide has been shown.
  • Whether Premium is active, as reported by Google Play.

1.6 What the App does not collect

The App does not collect your location, device identifiers, advertising ID, browsing data, call logs, SMS inbox, WhatsApp or Telegram chats, or any usage statistics. It does not ask you to create an account.

2. How the information is used

  • Sending: to open your messaging app, WhatsApp or Telegram with each recipient and message ready.
  • Scheduling: to remind you when scheduled messages are due (Premium).
  • History: to show what was sent, skipped or failed, and to let you send again.
  • Limits: to apply the 24-hour sending limits that help prevent spam.
  • Premium: to unlock Premium features when Google Play confirms your purchase.

Your information is never used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than the features you use.

3. Sending through other apps

The App does not send messages itself. Each message is sent by the app you choose, from your own phone number or account, under that app's own privacy policy:

  • SMS: your default messaging app and your mobile carrier.
  • WhatsApp: WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Telegram: Telegram Privacy Policy.

When a message is sent, the recipient, text and any attachments are handed to that app. What it does with them is governed by its policy, not ours.

4. Accessibility service (Automatic sending)

The App includes an optional Android accessibility service used only for "Automatic" sending on WhatsApp and Telegram. It is off until you turn it on. Before that, the App shows a clear explanation and asks for your consent.

  • Where it works: only inside WhatsApp and Telegram. It receives no information from other apps.
  • When it works: only while a send that you started is running, and only for the message currently being sent.
  • What it reads: the screen of WhatsApp or Telegram, only to find the message box and the Send button.
  • What it does: on Telegram it types your message into the message box; then it presses Send, and the App returns.
  • What it never does: it does not read, record, store, collect or share your chats, contacts or anything else shown on screen. It does not change any settings. Nothing it sees leaves your phone.
  • Your control: you can turn it off at any time in your phone's Settings → Accessibility, or from the App's "Check setup" screen. You can always send by pressing Send yourself instead.

6. Storage and security

  • All App data is stored in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • Android cloud backup is turned off for the App, so your data is not copied to any cloud backup by the App.
  • Attachments are shared with WhatsApp through a temporary, read-only permission for that message only.
  • Because the data never leaves your phone through us, keeping your phone locked and secure is the best protection for it.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information, and we do not have it: it is never sent to us. In particular:

  • Contacts are never shared with third parties.
  • Messages and attachments go only to the people you send them to, through the app you choose.
  • WhatsApp and Telegram chats are never accessed, stored or shared.

9. How long data is kept

  • Data stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • Deleting a send in History removes its recipients, messages and attachments.
  • Records used for the sending limits are cleared automatically after about two days.
  • Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, removes everything it stored.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Access: everything the App stores is visible in the App: your sends, recipients, messages and history.
  • Correction: fix your file or contacts and import them again.
  • Deletion: delete any send in History, or remove all data by uninstalling the App.
  • Permissions: allow or revoke Contacts, Notifications, Display over other apps and the accessibility service at any time.
  • Choice: contacts, attachments, scheduling and Automatic sending are all optional.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act or Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Decree give you rights over your personal data. Because we do not collect or hold your data, these rights are exercised directly on your phone as described above. You can still contact us with any request or question.

11. Data about the people you message

When you import or select recipients, you are responsible for having the right to use their contact details and for having their consent to receive your messages, under the laws that apply to you. The App keeps their details only on your phone and only for your sends.

12. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13, and the App does not send any information to us. If you believe a child has used the App, deleting its data or uninstalling it removes everything it stored.

13. International use

The App is available in many countries. Because your information stays on your phone, it is not transferred to other countries by us. Messages you send may be processed in other countries by your carrier, WhatsApp or Telegram, under their policies.
